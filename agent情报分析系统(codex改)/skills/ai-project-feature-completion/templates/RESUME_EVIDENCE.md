## Resume bullet template
`[Action] + [technical method] + [verified outcome] + [context/evidence]`

Do not invent users, uptime, latency, accuracy, cost savings, team size, production usage or awards. If the claim is not evidenced, rewrite it as a scope/implementation statement without a fabricated metric.
